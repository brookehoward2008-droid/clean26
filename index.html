Brooke Howard

work

I make things
from what I notice.

the strange shape of a shadow.
a sentence that will not leave.
light caught against something ordinary.

I like the space
between things.

order ↔ impulse

structure ↔ emotion

what I see ↔ what I remember

Sometimes an idea comes back as typography.
Sometimes a photograph. a book. a website.
Sometimes it comes back as something
I do not have a name for yet.

That is usually
the interesting part.

I came to design with a life already lived.
I bring curiosity, pattern, humor, precision,
and seven wrong versions before the one that breathes.

Know where the line is.
Then decide when to leave it.

I keep looking.
closer.
