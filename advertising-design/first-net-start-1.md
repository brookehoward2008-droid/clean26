## Retrieved production rules

- Black lines identify cut or trim.
- Green lines identify folds or scores.
- Red lines identify bleed.
- The intended bleed is 1/8 inch, or 0.125 inch = 9 points.
- Preserve the original construction before merging shapes or creating the offset-path bleed.
- The flattened net needs the required markings, the student's first and last name, and `#1`.
