Window Soul

A film and poem by Brooke Howard

HOW THE POEM ANSWERS THE JOURNAL PROMPT

At the beginning of this quarter, I wrote that success would mean being less afraid of the blank page, trusting my words, and developing a stronger voice. I did not expect the answer to arrive as a window. In his final message, my professor shared WindowSwap—someone else’s sky, tree, and light—and told us, “You did it,” before thanking us for our stories. Those words became the last part of this poem because they answered something I had been wondering all quarter: he had been reading. The first window was the distance I felt between myself and anyone trying to understand me; the second opened outward and let me see another person’s world. The poem became a song, then a film, bringing together the art, design, and healing I hoped to connect when this class began. I still overthink, and the blank page can still feel larger than it is, but I kept coming back. Not perfectly. But enough.
