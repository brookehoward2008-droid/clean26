Grid Layouts

Four pages on one 12-column grid. The geometry stays put; the colour, type and alignment change the feeling of each page, from blush to dusk, with touches of saffron throughout.

View the PDF Download
01 Blush

A soft blush field with a saffron label tab. The long quote reverses out of a vermilion band edged in yellow, and a small epigraph sits centred between the columns.

02 Night

The headline centres over the photograph, a deep teal band anchors the thumbnails, the italic quote leans right, and a saffron rule and tab catch the light.

03 Dusk

The whole page turns deep teal. Type reverses to white, the title and pull quote set right, and saffron subheads and rules glow against the dark.

04 Air

Pale teal mist under a saffron photo bar. A centred list, a right-set passage leaning toward the Space Needle, and a vermilion quote to close.
