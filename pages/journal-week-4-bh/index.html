Journal Week 4

The Hen in the Coop

In the straw-warm coop, where the dust turns gold, the little hen steps softly through her kingdom of feathers and feed. She is not grand, but she is busy with the sacred work of pecking, turning, listening, living. Her beak taps the earth like a tiny clock. Her wings hold the brown heat of morning. Every seed is a secret. Every shadow is watched.

Beside her stands the master rooster, red-crowned and proud, throwing his bright cry over the boards as if he pulls the sun up by its burning sleeve. He guards her with one black eye on the yard and one brave foot in the straw. He is ridiculous and royal at the same time. She does not bow to him, but she lets him believe the sky is his. Together they rule the coop: she with her quiet hunger, he with his thunderous throat, both alive in the small, loud miracle of morning.
