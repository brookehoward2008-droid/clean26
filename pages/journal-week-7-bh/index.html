The city curls into a wheel.
The road turns under itself.
Glass leans toward glass.
The sky lowers its face.

Do not hate the city.
It is only showing the mind:
round and round,
wanting, fearing, wanting.

Every car circles a center
it cannot enter.
Every window shines
with a person not at rest.

This is suffering,
not as punishment,
but as a hand on the shoulder
saying, look.

Hurry is thirst
wearing polished shoes.
It drinks from tomorrow
and is thirsty again.

It says, become more.
It says, arrive quickly.
It says, if you stop,
you will disappear.

But stopping
is not disappearing.
Sometimes stopping
is how the self returns.

Breathe.

A breath is a small doorway.
No crowd can take it.
No clock can own it.
Enter there.

See the buildings bend.
See the street roll on.
See the fear rise up
and pass like weather.

You are not the wheel.
You are not the traffic.
You are not the shadow
thrown by the tower.

You are the one who notices
the wheel turning.
You are the quiet witness
under the noise.

The small self you forgot
is not far away.
It waits like a lamp
behind a closed door.

Do not scold it
for being afraid.
Sit beside it.
Let it be held.

Let fear have a seat.
Let grief have a name.
Let anger unclench
its tired fist.

Nothing here is permanent:
not the spinning street,
not the sharp panic,
not the story you believed.

If I could advise the crowd,
I would say:
place both feet
on this passing earth.

Do one thing with kindness.
Answer one breath with another.
Leave one dream unfolded.
Let one silence speak.

The world may keep turning.
Let it turn.
You do not have to chase
every moving thing.

Listen.

In the center of the spin
there is a still place.
It is not far.
It is your own heart.

Slow down before you disappear
into the motion of wanting.
The road is turning,
but you may set down the load.
