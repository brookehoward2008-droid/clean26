Typography Layouts

Four magazine pages, one story, one set of paragraph and character styles, all built on the same 5 × 8 grid.

View the PDF Download

Grid

Page
600 × 840 pt, single pages, 0.125″ bleed
Margins
36 pt top and sides, 48 pt bottom
Guides
5 columns × 8 rows, 12 pt gutters, fit to margins
Baseline grid
12 pt. Every column and row lands on a multiple of 12.

Type

Title
Helvetica Neue LT Pro 97 Black Condensed, 96/84, all caps, tracking −10, optical kerning
Body text
Kepler Std Regular, 9.5/12, justified, aligned to the baseline grid
Subtitle
Kepler Std Semibold Italic Subhead, 21/24
Pull quote
Helvetica Neue Black Condensed, 24/24, with a 5 pt red rule

Styles

Paragraph styles
Title, Subtitle, Lead Paragraph, Body Text, Body Text Drop Cap, Section Subtitle, Bullets, Pull Quote, Footnote
Character styles
Bold, Italic, Accent, Drop Cap, Knockout, Lead-In, Lead-In Knockout
