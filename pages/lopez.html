ACTIVITIES

Lopez is the gentlest of the islands — flat cycling roads, friendly locals, tidal pools, and long views across the Salish Sea make it feel like a different world.

LODGING

From cozy farm stays to waterfront cottages, Lopez lodging keeps things unhurried and close to the land and sea.

DINING

The Bay Cafe and local farm stands bring fresh produce, seafood, and handmade everything to a community that still knows where its food comes from.
