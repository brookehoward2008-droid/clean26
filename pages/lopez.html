Discover the beauty of Lopez Island, the friendly island known for its rolling farmland.

Lodging

Looking for lodging on Lopez Island? Quiet farmstay inns, cozy cottages, and bayside cabins offer a peaceful retreat from busier islands.

Dining

Lopez dining is rooted in its farms and bakeries — Holly B's pastries, Lopez Island Creamery ice cream, and farm-to-table dinners at the Village.

Activities

Cycling the flattest of the San Juans, swimming at Spencer Spit, kayaking Fisherman Bay, and visiting the historical museum in the village.
