Activities

Hike & Explore

Moran State Park spans 5,252 acres with five freshwater lakes, over 30 miles of trails, and waterfalls. The summit of Mt. Constitution rises 2,409 feet — one of the finest panoramic views in the Pacific Northwest. Obstruction Pass offers the longest beach on Orcas, its crescent of marble-smooth rainbow pebbles unlike anything else in the islands.

Lodging

Stay Awhile

Deer Harbor village sits a scenic 20 minutes from the ferry and offers one of the few sandy beaches in the islands — plus marinas, B&Bs, and waterfront cabins. Doe Bay Resort is a rustic Northwest icon with soaking tubs, a sauna, and an organic garden steps from the water.

Dining

Farm to Table

Eastsound is the hub of the island — galleries, bakeries, a food co-op, and farm-to-table restaurants within easy walking distance. West Sound's Kingfish serves locally sourced ingredients with a view of Picnic Island. At Olga, the Lascaux Café sits inside a 1936 packing plant alongside the works of 45 Orcas Island artists.
