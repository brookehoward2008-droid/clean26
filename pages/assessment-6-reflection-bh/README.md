## Design direction

The layout treats the essay as a quiet psychological space rather than a standard academic page. It uses an almost-black field, bone-white text, muted micro-type, and a single gold thought-line. The title **A Window** comes from the essay’s central image: writing changing from a room of rules into a space where the writer can think and breathe.

Key visual ideas:

- dark `#0A0A0F` opening field
- subtle lift to `#0E0E14` through the body
- bone `#F2EDE4` for the title and final statement
- muted `#D4CFC5` body copy
- gold `#C4A265` thought-trace and emphasis
- deliberately generous whitespace around the fragmented-thinking section
- final line isolated at full brightness

The PDF was composed through Adobe and converted to an editable InDesign package so the layout can be refined directly in InDesign.
