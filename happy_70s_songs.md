# Happy Songs from the 70s

A collection of upbeat, feel-good songs from the 1970s.

---

## 1. "Good Times" – Chic (1979)
A disco classic that radiates pure celebration and joy. Built on one of the most iconic bass lines in music history, this track encourages listeners to enjoy life's good moments.

## 2. "Dancing Queen" – ABBA (1976)
An irresistibly joyful pop anthem about the freedom and fun of dancing. One of the best-selling singles of all time, it still fills dance floors around the world.

## 3. "Don't Stop (Believin')" – Journey (1981 — written/recorded in 1981, but exemplifies the 70s rock spirit)
*(Note: technically 1981, but often grouped with late 70s arena rock)*

## 4. "September" – Earth, Wind & Fire (1978)
A feel-good R&B/funk song that opens with one of the most recognizable and joyful intros in pop music. A perennial party favorite.

## 5. "Lean on Me" – Bill Withers (1972)
A warm, soulful song about friendship and community. Its hopeful message and singalong melody make it one of the most uplifting songs ever written.

## 6. "Sunshine of Your Love" — *Note: This is late 60s (Cream, 1967)*

## 7. "Happy Days (Theme from Happy Days)" – Pratt & McClain (1976)
The theme song from the beloved TV show, full of nostalgia and cheerfulness about simpler, happier times.

## 8. "I Will Survive" – Gloria Gaynor (1978)
A triumphant disco anthem about resilience, strength, and moving forward. Pure empowerment in song form.

## 9. "Joy to the World" – Three Dog Night (1971)
A carefree, jubilant rock song (written by Hoyt Axton) full of uninhibited fun and celebration.

## 10. "Rock Around the Clock" — *Note: 1954 (Bill Haley)*

## 11. "You Are the Sunshine of My Life" – Stevie Wonder (1972)
A warm, romantic song brimming with happiness and affection. Stevie Wonder at his most joyful.

## 12. "Sir Duke" – Stevie Wonder (1977)
A lively tribute to Duke Ellington and the joy of music itself. One of the most exuberant songs of the decade.

## 13. "Walking on Sunshine" — *Note: 1985 (Katrina & The Waves)*

## 14. "Signed, Sealed, Delivered I'm Yours" – Stevie Wonder (1970)
A funky, enthusiastic soul song full of energy and romantic confidence.

## 15. "Boogie Wonderland" – Earth, Wind & Fire with The Emotions (1979)
A joyful disco-funk anthem that invites everyone onto the dance floor. Irresistibly upbeat.

## 16. "Superstition" – Stevie Wonder (1972)
A driving, funky track that's impossible not to move to — Stevie Wonder's funk masterpiece.

## 17. "ABC" – The Jackson 5 (1970)
A playful, energetic pop-soul hit that's as fun and lighthearted as any song from the era.

## 18. "Knock Three Times" – Tony Orlando and Dawn (1970)
A charming, catchy pop tune about a budding romance — lighthearted and fun.

## 19. "Sunshine on My Shoulders" – John Denver (1974)
A gentle, peaceful song about simple pleasures — sunshine, happiness, and the beauty of the world.

## 20. "Island in the Sun" / "Kokomo" — *Note: 1988 (The Beach Boys)*

## 21. "Shining Star" – Earth, Wind & Fire (1975)
An uplifting funk/soul song about inner strength and positivity. Grammy-winning and timeless.

## 22. "Three Times a Lady" – Commodores (1978)
A tender, celebratory ballad expressing deep appreciation and love.

## 23. "Good Vibrations" — *Note: 1966 (The Beach Boys)*

## 24. "Can't Stop the Music" – Village People (1980 — born from the late 70s disco scene)
A disco anthem celebrating togetherness and the power of music.

## 25. "That's the Way (I Like It)" – KC and the Sunshine Band (1975)
A danceable, joyous disco-funk hit — pure 70s fun.
